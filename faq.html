Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you travel

How do I book a tour package?

Contact us via the enquiry form, WhatsApp, or email with your preferred destination and dates. Our travel expert will share a customized itinerary and quote within 24 hours. Confirm with a 30% advance payment to secure your booking.

What's included in the package price?

Most packages include accommodation, daily breakfast, private transfers, sightseeing as per itinerary, and monument entry fees where listed. Flights, lunches/dinners (unless specified), personal expenses, and travel insurance are typically not included.

Can I customize a package?

Absolutely! All our packages can be modified — change hotels, add/remove destinations, adjust duration, or build a completely custom itinerary. Just tell us your preferences during enquiry.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS), UPI, credit/debit cards, and Razorpay online payments. International guests can pay via PayPal or wire transfer.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation 30+ days before travel: 90% refund. 15–29 days: 50% refund. 7–14 days: 25% refund. Less than 7 days: no refund. See our full Cancellation Policy for details.

Do you provide travel insurance?

Travel insurance is recommended but not included by default. We can arrange comprehensive travel insurance for domestic and international travelers at competitive rates upon request.

Is it safe to travel with Unique Vacations?

Yes. We are a registered travel agency with licensed guides, verified hotel partners, and 24/7 on-trip support. All vehicles are insured and drivers are experienced professionals.

Do you handle group and corporate bookings?

Yes! We specialize in group tours, family reunions, corporate offsites, and incentive travel. Contact us for special group rates and dedicated account management.
