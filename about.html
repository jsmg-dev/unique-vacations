Our Story

Unique Vacations India was founded with a simple belief: every traveler deserves a journey as unique as they are. What started as a small tour operation in Rajasthan has grown into a trusted travel partner serving thousands of guests across India and abroad.

We don't just book trips — we craft experiences. From honeymooners seeking romance in Kerala's backwaters to adventure seekers conquering Ladakh's passes, our team of local experts designs itineraries that go beyond the ordinary.

Based in India with deep roots in every region we serve, we combine local knowledge with professional service to deliver vacations that create lasting memories.
